\section{Lean formalization of structural design invariance}
\label{app:lean-structural-design}
\providecommand{\LeanDecl}[1]{\texttt{\detokenize{#1}}}

\paragraph{Scope and assumptions.}
This appendix records exact real column-space results on a fixed finite sample.
The proof library uses Lean 4.19.0 and Mathlib commit
\texttt{c44e0c8ee63ca166450922a373c7409c5d26b00b}.
Verification is attached to an exact source commit passing the full build and
per-theorem dependency audit. It is not a claim about the Python planner.

\paragraph{Refinement and functional dependence.}
\LeanDecl{Refines} is defined by an actual level map on all sample rows.
\LeanDecl{partitionSpace_eq_span} identifies the categorical coefficient-map
range with the full indicator-column span. The dummy-space lemma is
\LeanDecl{partitionSpace_le}; equivalent partitions are handled by
\LeanDecl{equivalent_partition_spaces}. The dependency lemmas are
\LeanDecl{refines_trans}, \LeanDecl{refines_restrict} and
\LeanDecl{interaction_refines}. Restriction to fewer rows preserves a true
dependency; the statements do not certify a dependency inferred from a random
subsample or an unverified metadata claim.

\paragraph{Canonicalization and absorbed blocks.}
\LeanDecl{canonicalize_one} removes a coarse partition only while its fine
witness remains. \LeanDecl{absorbed_block_within_zero}, together with the
proved refinement inclusion, shows that a regressor block already contained
in the absorbed space has no residualized direction. The mathematical within
operator is constructed from existence and uniqueness of finite positive-weight
WLS; it is not introduced as an unspecified numerical routine.

\paragraph{Active and reference-coded columns.}
\LeanDecl{coarse_active_sum} requires completeness only for realized fine levels
inside the coarse cell being reduced. \LeanDecl{drop_active_fine} retains the
coarse indicator and all other fine columns needed for its sum identity.
This permits missing reference cells elsewhere, as well as unused labels,
but does not permit a further deletion when an observed fine cell required by
the identity is already absent. \LeanDecl{drop_one_fine} is the earlier
complete-block special case. Reductions in several coarse cells compose by
reapplying the same condition on each current active set.

\paragraph{Shared continuous multipliers.}
\LeanDecl{shared_multiplier_le} proves inclusion after multiplication by the
same realized continuous vector. \LeanDecl{drop_active_fine_multiplier} and
\LeanDecl{finite_reduction_common_multiplier} cover basis elimination and its
finite composition. The multiplier may vanish or be negative; the essential
condition is that it acts on both the fine columns and the retained coarse
witness. An unmultiplied coarse FE is not silently treated as a slope witness.

\paragraph{Main column-space theorem.}
\LeanDecl{StructuralStep} describes only the preceding mathematical reductions
and their common-multiplier and unchanged-context extensions. Its constructors
carry level maps and coverage conditions, not an assumed equality of the
spaces. \LeanDecl{structural_step_space_eq} proves equality for each case,
and \LeanDecl{structural_trace_space_eq} proves it for a finite sequence.
The term ``trace'' here denotes a finite mathematical proof composition,
not a formal model of the software planner's control flow.

\paragraph{OLS/WLS and coefficient semantics.}
\LeanDecl{wls_fit_exists_unique} proves existence and uniqueness of fitted
vectors; \LeanDecl{structural_trace_wls_fit} gives fitted-value invariance.
\LeanDecl{full_wls_implies_within} and
\LeanDecl{within_wls_implies_full} connect full and within WLS optima.
\LeanDecl{full_wls_identified_fe_invariance} therefore establishes equality of
identified non-FE coefficients when the FE spaces agree and the non-FE design,
sample and weights remain fixed.
For rank-deficient transformed designs, \LeanDecl{within_selection_invariance}
requires the same selection function of the same transformed design and outcome.
It does not equate minimum-norm coefficients of differently parameterized full
models. \LeanDecl{estimable_coefficient_fiber} handles coefficient functionals
annihilating the design kernel; \LeanDecl{estimable_fit_function_invariance}
handles a common linear functional of fitted values.

\paragraph{Finite PPML corollary.}
\LeanDecl{ppmlLoss} is the actual weighted Poisson objective, up to constants
independent of the predictor. \LeanDecl{ppml_midpoint_strict} proves strict
midpoint convexity for strictly positive weights, using strict convexity of
the exponential and cancellation of the linear response term.
\LeanDecl{finite_ppml_predictor_unique} proves uniqueness of an attained finite
predictor on the affine space determined by the design and offset.
\LeanDecl{finite_ppml_fitted_invariance} and
\LeanDecl{finite_ppml_response_invariance} give equal $\eta$ and $\exp(\eta)$
under equal design spaces, unchanged sample, weights, response and offset.
\LeanDecl{structural_trace_ppml} connects this to the actual allowed mathematical
reductions. Existence of a finite optimum is deliberately not asserted;
separation and numerical convergence are different questions.

\paragraph{Boundaries.}
No IV/GMM moment or covariance theorem is inferred from column-space equality
alone. Reference-restriction rules outside the stated witness conditions,
planner decisions, sampled certification, sparse data structures, numerical
ranks and floating-point behavior are not part of the mathematical claim.
The Lean results neither certify software implementations nor establish
historical novelty.
